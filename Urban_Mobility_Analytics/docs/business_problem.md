# Business Problems

## Project Objective

The objective of this project is to analyze historical urban mobility data
and identify demand patterns, geographic trends, and operational opportunities
that can support better transportation planning and decision-making.

---

## Business Questions

### 1. When does ride demand peak?
Identify the hours of the day with the highest and lowest pickup demand.

### 2. How does ride demand vary across different days of the week?
Compare pickup demand across weekdays and weekends.

### 3. How does ride demand change over time?
Analyze monthly and yearly trends in ride activity.

### 4. Which geographic areas have the highest ride demand?
Identify pickup hotspots using available geographic information.

### 5. How do ride patterns differ between weekdays and weekends?
Understand differences in mobility behavior between working days and weekends.

### 6. Which transportation providers have the highest demand?
Compare Uber and other available transportation providers based on recorded activity.

### 7. How does demand vary by transportation provider across different time periods?
Identify differences in peak hours, days, and months across providers.

### 8. Can unusual spikes or drops in ride demand be identified?
Detect abnormal demand patterns that may indicate unusual mobility behavior.

### 9. Can historical data be used to predict future ride demand?
Build a demand forecasting approach using historical temporal and geographic patterns.

### 10. How can predicted demand support better resource allocation?
Use demand forecasts to identify where and when additional transportation resources
may be required.
